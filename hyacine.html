Hyacine

Stats

Attack(ATK): 1449

Skills

Basic Attack: Deals Wind DMG equal to 30% of Hyacine's Max HP to one designated enemy.
Skill: Summons memosprite Little Ica, restores HP equal to 8% of Hyacine's Max HP plus 160 for all allies aside from Little Ica, and restores HP equal to 10% of Hyacine's Max HP plus 200 for Little Ica.
Talent: The memosprite Little Ica initially has Max HP equal to 50% of Hyacine's Max HP. When Hyacine or Little Ica provides healing, increases Little Ica's DMG dealt by 48% for 2 turn(s). Stacks up to 3 time(s).
Ultimate: Summons memosprite Little Ica, restores HP equal to 10% of Hyacine's Max HP plus 200 for all allies aside from Little Ica, and restores HP equal to 12% of Hyacine's Max HP plus 240 for Little Ica. Hyacine enters the "After Rain" state for 3 turn(s) afterward. Reduces the state's duration by 1 turn each time Hyacine's turn begins. When in "After Rain" state, increases Max HP for all allies by 30% plus 600.
